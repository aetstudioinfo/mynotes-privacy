MyNotes Privacy Policy

Thank you for using MyNotes (com.aetstudio.mynotes), developed by AET Studio. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how we protect your personal information. Please read it carefully before using the app.

In short: your notes stay on your device. They are sent to the cloud only if you sign in with a Google account and use the optional backup. Ads are shown by Google AdMob in the free version; crash reports go to Google Firebase Crashlytics and contain no notes.

  • In the app (immediate): Settings → Cloud Backup → Delete account & cloud data. Your account and all cloud backups are permanently deleted. Notes on your device are not affected.
  • By email: write to aetstudioinfo@gmail.com from the Google address you used in MyNotes. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Advertising and consent (AdMob / UMP)

  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and other regulated regions see a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested.
  • If you consent, your advertising ID may be used for personalised ads; if you decline, only limited (non-personalised) ads are shown.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → About → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • AdMob may share ad-related data (such as the advertising ID and ad interactions) with its advertising partners under Google's policies. We do not receive this data and we do not sell your personal information.
  • The "Remove Ads" purchase turns off all ads.

More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Google Firebase and cloud backup

  • You sign in with your Google account via Firebase Authentication. Only your email address and user ID are stored.
  • Each note is stored as a separate record in Google Cloud Firestore under your account. For drawing notes this includes the text and the pen strokes; photos are not uploaded.
  • If you turn on Automatic daily backup (Settings → Cloud Backup), your notes are backed up once a day. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Export notes (file) creates a file (notes and photos) in a location you choose on your device. This file is never sent to us.
  • Security rules ensure that only you can read or write your data; no other user can access it.
  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

More: Firebase privacy

Crash reports

  • If the app crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics sends us technical details: app version, device model, Android version and where in the code the error happened. It does not include your notes, photos or account information.
  • About once a week MyNotes also sends a size summary in ranges (for example "2-5 MB, 20-100 notes") through Crashlytics, so we can see whether notes are getting too large for fast saving and for Google's 25 MB automatic backup limit. It never contains note titles, text, photos, exact counts or account information.
  • If the app closed or froze last time, MyNotes may ask you to send a problem report. It is sent only if you tap Share, through the app you choose (e.g. email). It contains the same kind of technical details, never your notes.

Notifications and permissions

  • Notifications: reminders and notes pinned to the status bar
  • Exact alarms (Android 12+): on-time reminders (if not granted, reminders may arrive a few minutes late)
  • Run at startup: restoring your reminders after the device restarts
  • Internet: loading ads and cloud backup
  • Photos and camera: photos are chosen with the Android photo picker and taken with your camera app. MyNotes does not request access to your photo library or camera; it only receives the photos you pick or take.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock: used through Android for unlocking locked notes and the app lock. MyNotes never receives or stores your fingerprint.

Denying a permission only disables the related feature; all other functions keep working.

Data security

  • Local data is stored in Android's private app storage (DataStore).
  • Locked notes: the content of a locked note is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your PIN (PBKDF2). It stays encrypted on the device and in cloud backups. Note titles are not encrypted so they can be shown in lists.
  • How strong is a PIN? The protection is only as strong as your PIN. A 4-digit PIN keeps a locked note away from casual eyes, but someone who obtains a copy of your data (for example a shared backup file) could try every 4-digit combination. For sensitive notes, use a longer PIN (up to 8 digits) and keep your backup files private.
  • Locked drawing notes: the PIN is required to open the note and its text is encrypted, but its photos and pen strokes are not encrypted.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to any third parties.

Children

MyNotes is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, contact aetstudioinfo@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

Bildirimler ve izinler

  • Açılışta çalışma: cihaz yeniden başladığında hatırlatıcıların yeniden kurulması

Bir izni reddetmek yalnızca ilgili özelliği devre dışı bırakır; diğer tüm işlevler çalışmaya devam eder.

Veri güvenliği

  • Cihazdaki veriler Android'in uygulamaya özel depolama alanında (DataStore) saklanır.
  • Kilitli notlar: kilitli notun içeriği, PIN'inizden türetilen (PBKDF2) bir anahtarla AES-256-GCM ile şifrelenir; cihazda ve bulut yedeğinde şifreli kalır. Not başlıkları listelerde görünebilmesi için şifrelenmez.
  • PIN ne kadar güçlü? Koruma, PIN'iniz kadar güçlüdür. 4 haneli bir PIN kilitli notu meraklı gözlerden korur; ancak verilerinizin bir kopyasını ele geçiren biri (ör. paylaşılmış bir yedek dosyası) tüm 4 haneli olasılıkları deneyebilir. Hassas notlar için daha uzun bir PIN (8 haneye kadar) kullanın ve yedek dosyalarınızı kimseyle paylaşmayın.
  • Kilitli çizim notları: notu açmak için PIN gerekir ve yazısı şifrelenir; fotoğrafları ve kalem izleri şifrelenmez.
